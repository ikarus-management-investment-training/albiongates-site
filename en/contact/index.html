Albion Gate Advisory Ltd provides consultancy and coordination services. We connect clients with independent, regulated professionals and coordinate their work. UK immigration advice is provided exclusively by IAA-regulated advisers or immigration solicitors. Legal work in England and Wales is carried out by SRA-regulated solicitors or BSB-regulated barristers; Turkish legal and immigration matters are handled by lawyers registered with a Turkish bar association. We do not provide regulated financial, legal or immigration advice ourselves, and we do not hold or manage client money.
