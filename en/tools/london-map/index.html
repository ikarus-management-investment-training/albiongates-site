      How the map works
      Your destinations and everyday places

      Choose supermarket brands and add up to 3 regular destinations to personalise proximity scores. Distances are measured in a straight line from an area centre. A distance limit must be met for every saved destination. The safety filter uses a historical relative crime index; it does not describe route safety. Shops, stations and line geometry come from geographic snapshots rather than live services. Check a journey planner for routes and timings. The grocery proximity score decreases from 100 at 0 km to 0 at 5 km. Personal destination proximity uses the farthest destination, decreasing from 100 at 0 km to 0 at 20 km. These are this tool’s proximity scales.

      A starting point for your research.

      This map brings area indicators together around your priorities. Use it to build a shortlist, then check the places and services that matter to you in person.

      Your priorities shape the score

      The match score is a weighted average on a 0–100 scale. Higher weights give an indicator more influence; 0 excludes it. Missing indicators are left out of the average and reduce the data coverage shown. Relative standing compares a scored area with all other scored areas in the dataset. Searching or moving the map does not change that comparison group.

      Read the small-area boundaries

      The map covers 2021 Census Output Areas within roughly 10 miles (16 km) of central London. Each coloured shape is a small statistical area, not an entire neighbourhood. Place names help you orient yourself. The list groups results by ward; selecting a result opens a particular small area. A score should not be applied to every street or home in the wider district.

      Understand the historical snapshot

      Crime indicators use recorded incidents from 2023 to October 2025, weighted by category and adjusted for resident population. These are reference-map preferences, not official measures of harm. Reporting patterns, visitors and daytime population can affect comparisons. Crime data is excluded for 11 areas outside Greater London because their recent coverage is incomplete. Road-noise data describes 2022. These records do not guarantee current safety or living conditions.

      Check what the map cannot tell you

      Without personal destinations, transport uses the source data’s selected-line access index. With destinations, it uses straight-line proximity to the farthest destination. The map layer separately shows all 11 Tube lines. Grocery scores use proximity to OSM-listed supermarkets and convenience branches of your selected brands. Education combines historical qualifications and inspections, not school admissions. Rents, available homes, park access and air quality are not included.
