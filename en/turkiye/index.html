Planning an investment in Türkiye

What does a pre-feasibility report cover?

The agreed scope may compare sector requirements, candidate provinces, industrial locations, logistics, infrastructure and the relevant incentive framework. The report identifies questions for further investigation; specialist technical, legal and financial work may still be required.

Does historical incentive data show what my project qualifies for?

No. The 2019–2023 certificate dataset describes historical administrative records. Current eligibility depends on the rules in force and your project's specific facts, which must be assessed separately with the relevant professionals and authorities.

Can you compare organised industrial zones, free zones and technoparks?

Yes, within an agreed research scope. Their suitability depends on your activities, physical requirements and the rules applicable to each location. Any legal interpretation or application advice is provided by the appropriate Turkish professionals.

Who handles company formation and legal work?

Lawyers registered with a Turkish bar association handle Turkish legal and immigration matters. Qualified Turkish accountants handle accounting and tax work within their professional scope. We bring the team together and coordinate progress.

How are report fees and project costs agreed?

We define the research question and deliverables before preparing a quote. It separates Albion Gate's coordination or research fee, third-party professional fees, official charges and exclusions. Additional work requires an agreed change to scope.
