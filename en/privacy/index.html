Privacy

Your information, with a defined purpose.

This notice describes the functionality currently implemented on this website. The review items below must be completed before online enquiries are activated.

Who is responsible

The website is presented by Albion Gate Advisory Ltd. Controller identification, the privacy contact and the relevant contact details require review before launch.

Requires review before launch

Controller and privacy contact: to be confirmed.

What the forms will collect

Online enquiries are not active yet. When they are enabled, the contact form will ask for your name, email, optional phone number, intended market, topic, message and acknowledgement of this notice, and the report form will ask for company, sector, email and acknowledgement. Please do not submit passports, home addresses, children’s details or other sensitive information.

Online enquiries

This website does not currently collect form submissions. When online enquiries are enabled, your browser will send them directly to our enquiry service hosted in Türkiye, which will check them, limit automated submissions and store them there; they will not pass through the website host. This notice will be updated with the recipient, lawful basis and retention period before that happens.

Requires review before launch

Enquiry service recipient, lawful basis and retention period: to be confirmed before online enquiries are enabled.

Hosting and request records

This website has no database and does not receive form submissions. Its pages are served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub receives technical request information such as your IP address, browser details and the page requested, in order to deliver the site and protect its service, and handles it under its own privacy statement.

Requires review before launch

GitHub’s role, request-record retention and applicable safeguards: to be confirmed.

International transfers

GitHub delivers these pages through a global network, so request information may be processed outside the United Kingdom and Türkiye, including in the United States. The OpenStreetMap tile service and, if you choose them, Google Maps and WhatsApp links also involve providers outside Türkiye. Once online enquiries are enabled, enquiry data will be stored in Türkiye.

Requires review before launch

Processing locations, transfer arrangements and any applicable safeguards: to be confirmed.

Your display preferences

Two display controls store small values in your browser’s local storage. The quiet-view control stores an on/off preference under albion-quiet-view so the site remembers your motion preference. The appearance control stores light or dark under albion-theme when you choose Light or Dark; choosing Auto removes this value and the site follows your device setting. These values are not sent to the server. You can change them using the controls or remove them by clearing this site’s browser storage. No analytics or marketing tracking has been added to this application.

London map research

The London living map loads aggregate area indicators, boundaries and area labels from files hosted on this website. Scores and rankings are calculated in your browser. The map does not request your device location, send address searches to an external service, or require an account or personal-information form. You can optionally save up to three places of your own; how they are kept is described below. No map analytics have been added.

Your map preferences

The map stores your selected profile, seven priority weights and up to three saved area codes in your browser’s local storage under albion-london-map. These preferences are not sent to our server or stored in a map database. They remain in that browser until changed or removed. You can remove them by clearing this site’s browser storage.

Your places and map filters

The map’s everyday-context settings are stored separately in your browser’s local storage under albion-london-context: the grocery brands you select, which shop, station and line layers are shown, the maximum-distance and minimum-safety filters, and up to three personal places you add. A personal place consists of a generated identifier, the name you give it, its type (work, school or other) and map coordinates within London, which you type in or pick on the map.

These settings are used in your browser to show your places, calculate distances and filter areas. They are not sent to our server and are not included in sharing links. They do not expire automatically: they remain in that browser until you remove each place with its remove control or clear this site’s browser storage.

Because a place can reveal where you or a family member work or study, use a general name and avoid saving places on shared devices.

If you open a Google Maps route link, your browser sends Google the selected area’s reference coordinates and the coordinates of that place, together with the information involved in any visit to Google, such as your IP address and browser details; the link does not include the place’s name or type. Google handles that visit under its own privacy policy. Viewing or focusing on a saved place also loads background tiles for the surrounding area from OpenStreetMap, as described below.

Background map imagery

The background map loads tiles directly from tile.openstreetmap.org. The tile provider receives your IP address and requests identifying the map region you are viewing. These requests are needed to display the background map; the area indicators and preference calculations remain separate from this tile service.

Sharing a map view

When you choose to copy a sharing link, it includes your profile, priority weights and saved area codes in the part of the address beginning #living=. This URL fragment is not sent to our server when the page is requested. Anyone who receives the link can open those preferences. The link is copied only when you use the sharing control; the application does not send it to other people.
