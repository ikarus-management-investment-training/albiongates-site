Professional accountability

Know who is responsible for the advice

These bodies regulate or oversee the professionals we coordinate. Their names do not imply endorsement of Albion Gate.

SRA solicitor

The Solicitors Regulation Authority regulates solicitors and the firms it authorises in England and Wales.

BSB barrister

The Bar Standards Board regulates barristers and the legal services it oversees in England and Wales.

IAA immigration adviser

The Immigration Advice Authority regulates UK immigration advisers within their authorised scope.

ICAEW or ACCA accountant

Professional accountancy bodies set standards for their members. The relevant firm’s qualifications and permissions are checked for the agreed work.

Before you take the next step

Can I establish a company before moving to the UK?

We can coordinate an assessment of your proposed setup with the relevant professionals. Company formation, banking, tax residence and the right to live or work in the UK are separate questions; incorporating a company does not itself give immigration permission.

Can you help with banking and KYC?

We help organise the information and documents a bank may request. Each bank makes its own assessment, may ask for further evidence and decides whether to open an account. We cannot guarantee acceptance.

How long does a move or setup take?

Timing depends on the scope, the relevant authorities and third-party availability. Planning usually becomes clearer once documents are ready and the professionals have assessed the work. We agree a working schedule without promising an approval date.

Do you advise on immigration routes?

The eligibility assessment and immigration advice come from an IAA-regulated adviser or an immigration solicitor. We coordinate introductions, agreed administrative tasks and progress, including enquiries about Innovator Founder and Global Talent.

Can I use only part of the service?

Yes. Your agreed scope can cover a specific need, such as business setup or home-purchase coordination. The quote distinguishes Albion Gate’s coordination fee, third-party professional fees, official charges and exclusions.
