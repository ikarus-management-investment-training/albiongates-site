About Albion Gate

Good decisions need connected people.

Cross-border plans can stall when capable people work in isolation. Albion Gate exists to connect the conversations, responsibilities and next steps between the United Kingdom and Türkiye.

Book a free initial conversation

The gap is often coordination

A business plan can involve an accountant, a lawyer, a bank and a property specialist before it becomes a working business. A family’s move brings a different set of decisions, with just as many dependencies. We give those workstreams a shared plan and give you a clear point of contact.

Our role, clearly defined

Albion Gate Advisory Ltd provides research, consultancy and coordination for investment projects in the UK and Türkiye. We work with the independent specialists each project needs on sector reporting, investment feasibility, lobbying and stakeholder relations. We coordinate responsibilities and next steps within the agreed scope.

Professional advice comes from the relevant qualified or regulated specialist. We do not provide regulated financial, legal or immigration advice ourselves, and we do not hold or manage client funds.

The name

Albion + Gate

Albion is an ancient name for Britain. Gate expresses a point of connection: a considered way between two markets, with the right people alongside you.

The principles behind the work

Integrity

Clear professional boundaries, independent advice and honest explanations of what is known and what still needs checking.

Clarity

An agreed scope, visible responsibilities and a shared view of the next step, in English or Turkish.

Discretion

Information requested for a defined purpose and shared with the relevant people for the agreed work.

A few things worth making clear

What does Albion Gate do?

We carry out sector research and reporting, feasibility and stakeholder engagement work for investment projects. For establishment and settlement needs, we bring together independent specialists and coordinate documents and next steps through one English- and Turkish-speaking point of contact.

Are you a law firm or a regulated financial adviser?

No. Albion Gate provides consultancy and coordination services. Regulated financial, legal and immigration advice is provided by the appropriate independent professionals. We do not hold or manage client money.

Who provides immigration advice?

UK immigration advice comes exclusively from IAA-regulated advisers or immigration solicitors. Turkish legal and immigration matters are handled by lawyers registered with a Turkish bar association.

How are fees structured?

You receive one agreed scope and quote that separates Albion Gate’s coordination fee, third-party professional fees, official charges and exclusions. Each professional’s engagement terms and responsibilities remain clear. Any change in scope is agreed before additional work begins.

Can we communicate in Turkish throughout?

Yes. The coordination service is bilingual in English and Turkish. Where formal translations or certified documents are required, that work is identified separately in the agreed scope.

What should I prepare for the first conversation?

A short outline of your plans, the relevant market and the questions you want to resolve is enough to begin. Please do not send passports, home addresses or children’s details through the website forms.
